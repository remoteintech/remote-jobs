## Company blurb

datawebot gives AI agents ecommerce product data. Through one MCP server, an agent can search products across online shops worldwide, list each seller's price with a direct link to the shop, and run extraction Jobs on Amazon and Shopify stores. Anyone can sign in for free by approving an email.

## Company size

A small team of fewer than 10 people.

## Remote status

datawebot is a fully remote team. Every open role is remote and full time, and the team works asynchronously through GitHub issues and pull requests.

## Region

Hiring remotely across Asia; Asia time zones are preferred.

## Company technologies

* TypeScript and Python
* Cloudflare Workers and D1
* MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers
* AI coding agents such as Claude Code, Codex and Cursor

## How to apply

See the open roles at [datawebot.com/careers](https://www.datawebot.com/careers). To apply, email career@datawebot.com with the role title in the subject line, or read [datawebot.com/apply](https://www.datawebot.com/apply).
