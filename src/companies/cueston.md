## Company blurb

[Cueston](https://cueston.com/) is a user onboarding and product adoption platform for SaaS web apps. It gives a product two AI agents, one that demos the product live to website visitors and one that answers and guides signed-in users inside the app, together with product tours, tooltips, checklists, surveys, product analytics and session recordings.

## Company size

1-10 people.

## Remote status

Cueston is based in Warsaw, Poland, and its open roles are remote and full-time. The team is small, and coding agents (Claude Code, Codex) write most of the code, with people directing them and owning what ships. How the team works is described on the [careers page](https://cueston.com/careers).

## Region

Worldwide. A few shared hours with Central European working time help.

## Company technologies

- TypeScript, React and Next.js (App Router)
- PostgreSQL with hand-written SQL
- A JavaScript SDK that renders in Shadow DOM, and a Chrome extension (Manifest V3)
- REST API, MCP server and CLI

## How to apply

Open roles, pay ranges and the hiring steps are at [cueston.com/careers](https://cueston.com/careers).
