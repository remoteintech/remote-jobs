## Company blurb

Soshace Digital builds revenue systems that create and convert demand into qualified appointments for real estate and local service businesses. Its core offers are REX Chief of Staff for real estate inbound demand, Apex Booking System for local service inbound demand, and Pipeline Engine for managed outbound demand creation.

Current opportunities focus on independent contract sales partners who match qualified businesses with the appropriate system. Soshace Digital handles implementation, technical delivery, and ongoing client operations.
