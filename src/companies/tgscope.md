## Company blurb

[TGScope](https://tgscope.io) is an independent R&D lab and Telegram search engine. We index 3 million public Telegram channels, run semantic search and an interactive map on top of them, and publish research on the Telegram ecosystem.

## Company size

11-50 people.

## Remote status

Fully remote, there is no office. Work is asynchronous and written-first. Research and engineering roles are project-based contracts paid by the hour.

## Region

We hire in North America and Europe.

## Company technologies

Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Qdrant, ONNX Runtime, DuckDB, Next.js.

## How to apply

Open roles and the application form are on our [careers page](https://tgscope.io/careers).
