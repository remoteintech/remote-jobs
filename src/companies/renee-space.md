## Company blurb

Renée Space is an AI therapy chat app that helps people feel heard, understand themselves better, and navigate life's challenges with more confidence. We pair clinically reviewed guidance with Renée, an AI companion that listens, reflects, and helps people take the next small step — alongside journaling, behavioral pattern insights, and daily check-ins on web and mobile.

## Company size

We're a small, remote-first team where your work is visible and ships quickly.

## Remote status

Fully remote from the start — work from anywhere in the world. We're async-first with flexible hours: we care about outcomes, not time zones.

## Region

Worldwide.

## Company technologies

* TypeScript, React, Next.js
* Flutter (mobile)
* Firebase (Firestore, Cloud Functions)
* LLM integrations and RAG (ChromaDB)
