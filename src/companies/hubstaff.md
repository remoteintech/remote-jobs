## Company blurb

Hubstaff is a time tracking and workforce management platform that helps businesses monitor productivity, manage schedules, and process payroll for distributed teams. Founded in 2012, the company serves over 112,000 businesses with products built entirely by their fully remote team spanning 28 countries.

Hubstaff operates as a fully remote company with team members spread across the globe.

## Remote status

Hubstaff is a fully remote company. The entire team works on a distributed basis from various countries and locations worldwide, with a strong culture built around asynchronous communication and flexible working hours.
