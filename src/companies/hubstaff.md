## Company blurb

Hubstaff is a time tracking and workforce management platform that helps businesses monitor productivity, manage schedules, and process payroll for distributed teams. Founded in 2012, the company serves over 112,000 businesses.

## Remote status

Hubstaff is fully remote from day one, with products built entirely by a distributed team spanning over 28 countries.
