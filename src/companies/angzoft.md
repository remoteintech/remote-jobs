## Company blurb

Angzoft is an Indonesian software company that develops ERP solutions for distributors and retailers. Its platform brings sales orders, inventory and warehouse operations, delivery, invoicing, human resources, payroll, and accounting together in one system. Angzoft also builds custom web and mobile applications for businesses.
