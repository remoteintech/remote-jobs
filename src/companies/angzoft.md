## Company blurb

Angzoft is an Indonesian software company that develops ERP solutions for distributors and retailers. Its platform brings sales orders, inventory and warehouse operations, delivery, invoicing, human resources, payroll, and accounting together in one system. Angzoft also builds custom web and mobile applications for businesses.

## Company size

Angzoft has a team of 2 people.

## Remote status

Angzoft's software team is fully remote, with no office attendance requirement. Work is coordinated through written scopes, Git, messaging, and video calls. Angzoft hires directly for its own software work and is not a staffing or recruitment agency.

## Region

Angzoft hires in Indonesia.

## Company technologies

Angzoft works with Laravel, PHP, JavaScript, and Node.js.

## Office locations

None. The team works remotely.
