## Company blurb

First Point is a product studio building mobile apps, AI agents and blockchain tools.

We ship consumer iOS and Android apps under our own name, including Longy (a sauna and cold
plunge tracker), BlueRadar (finds lost Bluetooth devices), Cleaner Wave (speaker water eject
and audio utilities), HeartRateMonitor (pulse readings from the phone camera), NFC Tool,
Cosmo Puzzle and SimpleJack. Alongside the apps we build AI agent tooling and blockchain
infrastructure.

## Company size

Fewer than 10 people.

## Remote status

Fully remote. We have no offices and the team works across time zones.

## Region

Worldwide

## Company technologies

React Native, Swift, React, TypeScript and Node.js.
